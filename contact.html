Quick answers

Before you send.

Is the growth audit really free?

Yes. There is no fee. You keep the findings whether or not we work together.

Will I need to share any reports?

Sometimes, yes. Depending on your business, a few reports may help us give you sharper findings. You don't need to prepare anything now. Our team will contact you at the right time and tell you exactly what would help.

How does a growth partnership work?

A small base fee keeps the work running, and our main earnings come from Growth Share: we earn more only when your agreed results grow. Everything is agreed in writing before we start.

Is my information safe with you?

Your privacy matters to us. Everything you share is used only to understand your business and get back to you. It stays confidential, and we never share or sell it to anyone.
