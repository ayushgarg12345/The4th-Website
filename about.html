Our story

Great products don't sell themselves. Not anymore.

0% read

It started inside a small brand, not a boardroom.

In 2026, during my marketing internships, I worked inside a young D2C brand and a legal-tech startup. I ran their ads, wrote their content and tracked every rupee they spent. And I kept seeing the same thing.

The products were good. The founders were working harder than anyone I had met. They had put their savings, their evenings and their reputation into what they built. And still, the sales didn't come the way they should have.

They didn't lack effort. They lacked support.

There was no real team behind them, no time to study who their customers were and why they bought, and the marketing help they had tried before had stayed on the surface.

When I asked why they didn't bring in an agency, the answer was almost always the same. They had tried. They had paid retainers, received reports full of clicks and impressions, and watched their money disappear while nobody took responsibility for the result. Many had stopped trusting anyone outside the business with their growth.

That stayed with me. These founders didn't need more marketing. They needed someone on their side: someone who would understand the business as deeply as they did, and only win when they won.

What we learned

0+

Brands studied before THE4TH began

So before starting THE4TH, we went looking for the pattern. We studied more than 1,000 brands: how they position themselves, how they advertise, how their websites sell and how they keep customers. A few practical truths came up again and again.

Truth 01

It's rarely the ads.

Most growth problems are not advertising problems. They sit in positioning, the offer, the website or what happens after the first purchase.

Truth 02

Trust is broken.

Founders don't trust agencies, and often for good reason. Many have paid for reports full of clicks and impressions while sales stayed flat.

Truth 03

Agencies stay outside.

They see ad dashboards, not the business, so they can only improve what they are shown.

Truth 04

Short cycles.

Too many agencies think in short cycles. The aim is to sell the next service or renew the retainer, not to build something that lasts.

That is why THE4TH exists

Not as another agency, but as a growth partner that works from inside your business.

We study your customers, find what is really holding sales back, then build and run the system that fixes it. We think in years, not retainers, and because we earn through Growth Share, we only win when you do.

Turiya · The philosophy behind THE4TH

Three states everyone knows. One that sees them all.

The Mandukya Upanishad, an ancient Sanskrit text, describes three familiar states of experience: waking, dreaming and deep sleep. Beneath them is a fourth, Turiya, the awareness that observes all three.

We take this as a way of seeing, not a spiritual claim. In business, most people work on the surface. We look for the fourth layer: the system underneath that decides whether growth happens.
