Our approach · The THE4TH Growth System

Four stages. One connected growth system.

Most growth problems get diagnosed in the wrong place. Rising ad costs are often a conversion problem. Flat sales are often a retention problem. We work through four stages in order, so we fix the cause, not the symptom. Select a stage to see inside it.

Stage 01

Discover

Find the real constraint.

Sound familiar?

You've changed agencies, tried new creatives and raised budgets, and the numbers still won't move.

What's really happening
10,000 visit
300 add to cart
90 buyThe leak

Picture a common funnel: 10,000 people visit, 300 add to cart, 90 buy. Most businesses respond by buying more visitors. But if the weak step is the 300 → 90, doubling traffic also doubles the leak. Fixing the weakest step lifts every number after it.

What we do

Before touching a single campaign, we find that one constraint and build the plan around it.

Feeds into → Acquire

Stage 02

Acquire

Win customers you can afford.

Sound familiar?

Your dashboard says the ads are working, but your bank account doesn't agree.

What's really happening
Your ads dashboard3× ROAS₹2,400 order from ₹800 of ads
Your bank account−₹200₹600 profit − ₹800 ad cost

Say a new customer costs you ₹800 in ads and places a ₹2,400 order. The dashboard proudly shows 3× ROAS. But after product cost, shipping, fees and returns, that order may leave only ₹600 of profit, so every "successful" sale loses ₹200. A good-looking ROAS can still be losing you money.

What we do

We judge every channel by what a customer costs against what they're actually worth, cut what doesn't pay back, and scale what does.

Feeds into → Convert

Stage 03

Convert

Stop paying for visitors who leave.

Sound familiar?

Traffic is up, sales aren't. People add to cart and disappear.

What's really happening
7 in 10 carts abandoned
+33%3 buyers in 100, then 4

About 7 in 10 online shopping carts are abandoned before payment, according to Baymard Institute's research. And the maths of fixing it is powerful: if 3 in 100 visitors buy today, getting just 1 more to buy grows sales by 33%, without spending a rupee more on ads.

What we do

We find exactly where interested people drop off, and remove the reasons they leave.

Feeds into → Compound

Stage 04

Compound

Make every customer worth more.

Sound familiar?

You're always chasing new customers and running another sale, while margins keep shrinking.

What's really happening
−₹2001 order
+₹4002 orders
+₹1,0003 orders

Take the customer from Stage 02: ₹800 to win, ₹600 profit per order. Buy once, and you've lost ₹200. Buy three times, and you've made ₹1,000 from the same ₹800. Research by Bain & Company found that a 5% increase in customer retention can lift profits by 25% to 95%.

What we do

We build the follow-ups, journeys and reasons to return, so every month starts from a stronger base than the last.
