The partnership model

We don't believe in disconnected incentives.

Your growth needs more than a vendor relationship. It needs shared accountability, transparent measurement and a partner invested in the outcome.

Part 01

Operating partnership

You fund the agreed operating costs to build and run the growth system.

  • Strategy and execution
  • Team and specialist resources
  • Technology and tools
  • Agreed operating requirements

Advertising spend is budgeted and approved by you, separately.

Part 02

Performance participation

THE4TH earns an additional fee linked to a measurable commercial outcome we agree on together, before work begins.

  • Based on collected revenue, eligible orders or another agreed metric
  • Attribution method written down upfront
  • Full reporting access for both sides

Core belief

Our incentives should move in the same direction.

You invest in the agreed operations. We participate in the growth we help create.

Every agreement defines, in writing

  1. What counts as an attributable conversion
  2. Revenue, orders or other agreed metric
  3. Attribution methodology
  4. Refunds and cancellations
  5. Repeat purchases
  6. Reporting access
  7. Client responsibilities
  8. Payment timing

We don't guarantee growth. Sales depend on product, pricing, fulfilment and many things outside any partner's control. What we commit to is clear deliverables, honest measurement and a fee that rises only when agreed results do.

Selective partnerships

One market.One growth partner.

We aim to work with one client in a clearly defined competitive market, so we never sit on both sides of the same customer.

The market boundary is agreed together, by product category, customer segment and geography. It's a commitment to focus and trust, not artificial scarcity.

Turiya · The philosophy behind THE4TH

Why the fourth.

Turiya comes from Sanskrit philosophy: the fourth state of consciousness described in the Mandukya Upanishad, beyond the three familiar states.

We borrow it as a metaphor, not a method. For us it means stepping back from the familiar view to see the whole. In business, that's looking past marketing activity to the system that produces growth.
